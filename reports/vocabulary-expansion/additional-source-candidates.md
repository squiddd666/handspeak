# Additional source candidates — metadata only

WLASL, MS-ASL, ASL Citizen, and ASL-LEX/SignBank metadata have candidates for 185 catalog entries, including **53 of the 66** previously missing local-source entries. No videos were downloaded or trained in this source audit. These are source leads, not verified semantic mappings or enabled recognition words.

New leads: ALLERGY, AMBULANCE, ASSIGNMENT, BABY, BAG, BORED, BREAKFAST, CHARGER, CHICKEN, CLASSROOM, CONFUSED, DINNER, DON'T UNDERSTAND, EIGHTEEN, EVENING, EXAM, EXCUSE ME, FIFTEEN, FIVE, FORGET, FOURTEEN, GLASS, GOOD AFTERNOON, GOOD MORNING, HOSPITAL, HOW MANY, HOW MUCH, INJURY, LUNCH, MEAT, MONEY, NERVOUS, NINETEEN, OKAY, PARENT, QUESTION, REMEMBER, REPEAT, RICE, RUN, SEVENTEEN, SIXTEEN, SURPRISED, TEN, THIRTEEN, THOUSAND, TWELVE, TWENTY, WALK, WELCOME, WOMAN, WORRIED, YOU'RE WELCOME.

The remaining 13 entries without a text/synonym candidate in this comparison: GOOD EVENING, FEVER, STOMACHACHE, PHARMACY, CLASSMATE, POLICE STATION, MARKET, TAXI, DON'T TOUCH, UNSAFE, CALL POLICE, CALL AMBULANCE, DON'T HAVE.

Counts reflect metadata, not accessible videos. Check signer identities across partitions and datasets, unknown IDs, duplicate clips, regional variants, and ambiguous English glosses before training. Source clips and model distribution must comply with the dataset terms; no new source assets are published here.

Source: [official WLASL repository](https://github.com/dxli94/WLASL), [dataset description and C-UDA license reference](https://github.com/dxli94/WLASL/blob/master/index.md). Only SignBank lexical translations are used; nondominant naming responses are excluded because they may be guesses rather than equivalent signs. These translations can be ambiguous and are never used to relabel training data automatically. The companion JSON records the metadata hash and per-word partition counts.
