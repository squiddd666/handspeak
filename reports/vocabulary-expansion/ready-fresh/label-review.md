# Label-review lead: PEN / PENCIL

The frozen fresh-recording check accepted PENCIL for three PEN clips. This is
an error under the declared label contract; the test scores were not changed.

ASL University describes PEN as commonly fingerspelled, and its PENCIL entry
notes a writing-tool interpretation can depend on context:

- https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/p/pen-writing.htm
- https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/p/pencil.htm

This is a reason to review dataset variants and the intended website lesson,
not proof the dataset clips are mislabeled or interchangeable. Do not merge
labels, relabel examples, or count the observed confusion as correct without
an explicit linguistic review and a new, disclosed evaluation protocol.
